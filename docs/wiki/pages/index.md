**Rundesk CLI** turns a coding-agent CLI already on a Mac into a named agent with a home, its own records,
and a gateway that keeps it reachable after the terminal closes.

The [brief](brief.md) says in one screen what Rundesk CLI is, who it serves and what it refuses.
[Installing](installing.md) puts it on a machine, [the install root](install-root.md) says where
everything it holds lives, and [commands](commands.md) lists every verb.

An [agent](agents.md) is the thing a person names and talks to, and its [gateway](gateways.md) is the
process that keeps it answering. A [provider](providers.md) runs the model behind it, a
[channel](channels.md) carries a conversation from Discord or Slack, a [schedule](schedules.md) starts
work without being asked, and a [skill](skills.md) is what an agent is taught. [Asking](asking.md)
follows one question from a terminal to its answer.

The [goals page](goals.md) collects what every part of Rundesk CLI is for.
